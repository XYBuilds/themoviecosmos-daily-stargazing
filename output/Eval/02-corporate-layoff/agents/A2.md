# A2 · The Sociologist · 02-corporate-layoff

## 伪剧情（英文 · 3 pseudos）

### p1
- **fragments**: why-0, why-1, why-2

A strategic reinvention, framed as an acceleration toward an AI-driven future, masks a classic capital-labor rebalancing. The imperative to co-locate teams in select hubs is an act of spatial consolidation, where the physical footprint of labor is contracted to better serve the abstract demands of capital. The workforce becomes a variable to be optimized, its dispersal or consolidation dictated by the latest logic of technological accumulation.

### p2
- **fragments**: how-0, how-1, how-2, how-3

The apparatus of strategic realignment is set in motion by executive fiat. A directive from the apex of the corporate hierarchy initiates the elimination of three thousand roles, a number that renders individual tenure a mere administrative detail. The offer of severance—a calculated buyout of time—accompanies the winding down of peripheral offices, the physical dissolution of local labor nodes.

### p3
- **fragments**: result-0, result-1

The result is a material redistribution of resources and security. Approximately three thousand full-time positions are excised, a direct transfer of risk from the corporate entity to the individual worker. Concurrently, two geographic offices enter dissolution, severing the link between community and corporate presence. The final ledger shows a leaner structure for capital and a dispersed, precarious cohort for labor.
