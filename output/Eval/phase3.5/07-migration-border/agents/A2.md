# A2 · The Sociologist · 07-migration-border

## 伪剧情（英文 · 3 pseudos）

### p1
- **fragments**: why-0

The seasonal tempering of weather, a force indifferent to policy, draws human movement toward the frontier. This natural impulse is met not by a porous system but by a calibrated apparatus of detention. The state's response is not to the individuals but to the statistical correction their arrival represents, transforming climate into a metric of enforcement intensity.

### p2
- **fragments**: how-0, how-1

The state's counting of bodies at the border constitutes a resource allocation contest. A rise in detentions from 6,598 to 8,268 within a month is not a humanitarian event but a fiscal pressure point, a numerical argument in a legislative standoff where the flow of people becomes leverage in a struggle over funding for their own control.

### p3
- **fragments**: result-0, result-1, result-2

The legislative stalemate over enforcement funds reveals the core conflict: the periphery (the border) generates a crisis, while the center (the capital) refuses to price the response. The migrant, caught between the desert crossing and the detention cell, is the silent object over which the powerful debate the cost of containment.
