# A2 · The Sociologist · 06-tech-monopoly

## 伪剧情（英文 · 3 pseudos）

### p1
- **fragments**: why-0

The digital marketplace is structured to favor the vertically integrated platform, where the owner of the infrastructure can steer traffic toward its own commercial units. This self-preferencing creates a systemic resource gap, channeling visibility and revenue away from rival comparison services—smaller entities that lack the structural power to contest their marginalization within the search results they depend on.

### p2
- **fragments**: how-0, how-1

The regulatory apparatus, centered in the metropolitan core of European power, subjects a dominant global corporation to a slow, procedural reckoning. The multi-year proceedings and the rejection of the firm's own proposed remedies reveal a contest between the rule-making authority of the supranational state and the evasive maneuvering of transnational capital, where the latter's preferred timelines and solutions are subordinated to the former's institutional rhythm.

### p3
- **fragments**: result-0, result-1

The anticipated record fine is less a singular penalty than the latest accumulation in a ledger of financial sanctions spanning more than a decade. This pattern reveals a structural tension: the fine is a significant but manageable cost for the corporate center, a tax on its market dominance, while for the peripheral rivals it sought to disadvantage, the damage to their competitive position remains unquantified and unresolved.
