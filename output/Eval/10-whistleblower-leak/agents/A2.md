The coaching industry, which fattens itself on the scarcity of seats and the desperation of families, produces figures who publicly perform the role of student champion while privately selling the paper itself — a neat synthesis of profiteer and patron. The whistleblower who exposed the breach arrives from the same coaching epicenter, revealing how tightly the apparatus of access and the apparatus of subversion are fused.
