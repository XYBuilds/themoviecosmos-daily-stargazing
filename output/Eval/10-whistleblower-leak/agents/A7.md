The entire edifice collapses because of one email — a single message from a small coaching town, carrying PDFs that replicate the chemistry paper question for question. And the deepest irony: among the thirteen arrested is a private-tutoring figure who, two years earlier, had stood before cameras and loudly demanded justice for students cheated in the previous year's leak. He had championed fairness so convincingly that he was trusted with access — the very access that undid the exam.
