# A2 · The Sociologist · 10-whistleblower-leak

## 伪剧情（英文 · 1–3 pseudos）

### p1
- **fragments**: why-0

The distribution of a medical entrance examination's content before the test constitutes a direct assault on the principle of meritocratic sorting. The leak originates from within the institutional apparatus responsible for gatekeeping, creating a resource gap between those with illicit advance access and the mass of candidates whose futures depend on the exam's presumed integrity. The state's response—canceling the test and initiating a criminal investigation—frames the act as a deviation from the norm, yet the norm itself is a mechanism for allocating scarce social positions, here temporarily subverted by a breach in the chain of custody.

### p2
- **fragments**: how-0, how-1, how-2, how-3

The sequence from examination to exposure reveals the structure of bureaucratic accountability. A central agency administers a nationwide test; an individual whistleblower provides evidence; the agency transfers suspicion to its own contracted workforce. The arrest of thirteen, including a figure previously aligned with student advocacy, underscores the precarious position of labor within the system: those who construct the instrument of selection can themselves become subjects of its punitive mechanisms when the center's control is breached.

### p3
- **fragments**: result-0, result-3

The cancellation of the examination and its rescheduling represent a rupture in the normal flow of credentialization. For the governing body, it is a restoration of procedural legitimacy. For the examinee cohort, it is a forced re-entry into a high-stakes, anxiety-producing process. The arrests signal the center's capacity to discipline its periphery, while the rescheduled date reimposes the timeline of institutional power upon millions of individual ambitions.
