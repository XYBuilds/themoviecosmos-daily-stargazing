# A2 · The Sociologist · 01-grid-outage

## 伪剧情（英文 · 1–3 pseudos）

### p1
- **fragments**: why-0, why-1, how-1

The seasonal surge in demand—driven by the heat endured by the island population—collides with the fragility of a privatized generation fleet. When a single corporate unit trips, the operational margin for the entire archipelago vanishes. The system's vulnerability is not random but a direct consequence of a structure where the profit motives of power companies dictate the reliability of a public good.

### p2
- **fragments**: how-0, how-1, how-2, how-3, result-2

A cascade of generator failures reveals the thin margin upon which the regional grid operates. The decision to impose emergency load shedding is a managerial act that distributes scarcity. The command flows from officials to the grid operator, translating a technical fault into a political allocation: whose lights go out, and whose stay on, is determined by the grid's topology, which itself reflects historical investment patterns and population density.

### p3
- **fragments**: result-0, result-1, how-2

The archipelago enters a red alert, a formal state of emergency defined by the risk of rotational blackouts. This condition is the materialized resource gap: the capacity to generate power falls critically short of the capacity to consume it, a deficit borne entirely by the end-users. The grid's precariousness becomes a lived reality, not an abstract metric, imposed upon the population by a system operating on the brink.
