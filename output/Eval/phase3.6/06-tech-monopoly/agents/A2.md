# A2 · The Sociologist · 06-tech-monopoly

## 伪剧情（英文 · 1–3 pseudos）

### p1
- **fragments**: why-0, result-0

The digital gatekeeper, whose algorithmic architecture is the market's primary infrastructure, favors its own commercial tendrils. This self-preferencing is not a glitch but a structural dividend of dominance, converting a public-facing utility into a private profit engine. The penalty, though substantial, is merely the cost of operating a system where the rules are written by the market's center against its periphery. The regulator, as the institutional counterweight, can only tax the excess, not dismantle the architecture itself.

### p2
- **fragments**: how-0, how-1, how-2

A multi-year procedural gauntlet, defined by the regulator's formal rejection of the corporation's proposed remedies, frames the exercise of state power. The corporation, with the resources to endure prolonged negotiation, views the process as a contest of attrition. The formal decision, timed to a bureaucratic calendar, represents the state's attempt to impose its temporal authority over the capital's indefinite timeline. The gap is not in resources, but in the fundamental power to set the rhythm of accountability.

### p3
- **fragments**: result-0, result-1

The fine is quantified in the hundreds of millions, yet it accrues to a ledger already bearing a decade of such penalties. For the dominant platform, these are sequential compliance costs, not existential threats. The regulator's victory is financial and symbolic, a recurring extraction from the monopoly rent. For the ecosystem of smaller competitors whose services were demoted, the structural advantage remains untouched; the penalty is paid to the state, not redressed to them. The resource gap between the ruler and the ruled of the digital realm persists.
