A junior data scientist, eager to impress, typed 'AI-driven product work' into the subject line instead of 'A.I. — Ackerman & Interns.' The phrase bounced up the chain to a VP skimming messages on a treadmill, who read it as a mandate. Months later, when the CEO announced the layoffs of 3,000 employees, the internal memo cited 'accelerating AI-driven product work' as the reason. No one remembers the original typo, or the interns' aborted kickball league.
