# A2 · The Sociologist · 02-corporate-layoff

## 伪剧情（英文 · 1–3 pseudos）

### p1
- **fragments**: why-0, how-0, how-1

The rationale for restructuring, articulated from the executive suite, frames the elimination of thousands of positions as a strategic necessity to accelerate AI-driven growth. This narrative justifies the dissolution of existing roles by positioning capital's pursuit of technological dominance over the immediate security of labor, rendering the workforce a variable cost to be optimized in service of a future-oriented vision conceived by the ruling class.

### p2
- **fragments**: how-0, how-1, how-2

The operational mechanism of the restructuring concentrates decision-making power at the top, where a single executive communicates the restructuring to staff, while the prescribed severance—a formulaic 16 weeks plus tenure-based increments—serves as a formalized transaction for the commodified labor it displaces. This process underscores the power gap between the architects of change, who design the terms of displacement, and the affected employees, who must accept the defined terms of their exit.

### p3
- **fragments**: result-0, result-1, result-3

The result is a stark material redistribution: approximately one-sixth of the workforce is cut, a quantitative shedding of human capital deemed non-essential for the new strategic direction. The winding down of specific offices in Reno and Woodland Hills illustrates the geographic and infrastructural footprint of this capital reallocation, where the periphery is sacrificed to consolidate resources toward a centrally-defined technological future.
