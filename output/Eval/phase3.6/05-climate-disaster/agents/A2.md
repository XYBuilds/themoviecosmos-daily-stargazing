# A2 · The Sociologist · 05-climate-disaster

## 伪剧情（英文 · 1–3 pseudos）

### p1
- **fragments**: why-0, how-2

A historical rainy season, a climatic event beyond local control, exposed the structural vulnerability of the periphery. The downstream population, living in the agricultural zones of Deir Ezzor and Raqqa, bore the full consequence of upstream decisions and systemic neglect. Their homes and schools were sacrificed to a hydrological logic dictated by distant reservoirs and seasonal patterns they did not command.

### p2
- **fragments**: how-0, how-1, how-2, how-3, how-4

The crisis was mediated by a cascade of infrastructure: reservoirs filled to capacity, earthen embankments failed, and a bridge collapsed. This infrastructure, built to manage resources, instead became the vector of their violent redistribution. The state's response—a naval deployment for evacuation—confirmed the scale of failure, positioning the population as subjects of rescue rather than citizens with a right to security.

### p3
- **fragments**: result-0, result-1, how-5

The outcome was a stark negotiation between states, with Turkey, the upstream power, agreeing to reduce releases. This geopolitical settlement occurred only after the downstream periphery was already submerged. The drowned children and collapsed bridge constitute the uncounted cost of this resource diplomacy, their deaths a footnote in a treaty over water flow.
