# A2 · The Sociologist · 04-celebrity-scandal

## 伪剧情（英文）
A channel of near-one-million subscribers deploys synthetic speech and fabricated evidence against a figure whose body is itself a commodity — endorsements, public appearances, marketable aura — and in a single news cycle that commodity collapses. The instrumentalized figure at the center, a woman already dead, belongs to neither side yet is spoken for by both; she is the purest object in the exchange. The state intervenes not at the moment of reputational ruin but at the moment the ruin is proven financially motivated, revealing whose losses count as crimes.
