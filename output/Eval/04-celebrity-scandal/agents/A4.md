## 伪剧情（英文）
A voice-master in the great marketplace conjures speeches never uttered and letters never written, binding them with the craft of the age. He knows the truth — that the bond between the star and the dead girl began only after she had crossed the threshold of womanhood — yet he sets the false seal upon his words, for the crowd pays well for outrage. The accused's name darkens; patrons flee; he vanishes from the public stage. But it is an old law that the weaver of false voices shall be seized by the very threads he spun, and the magistrate tears the conjurer's mask away.
