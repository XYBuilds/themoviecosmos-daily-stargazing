## 伪剧情（英文）
Police in a major city secure an arrest warrant for the operator of a channel with a large following. The operator allegedly used fabricated audio and manipulated message screenshots to claim that a well-known actor had dated a former co-star while she was underage. Prosecutors say the operator knew the relationship began only when the co-star was an adult but spread the false claims to generate revenue. The accusations had caused the actor to lose advertising deals and cancel public appearances for months.
